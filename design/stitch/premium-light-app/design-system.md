## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a "quiet luxury" boutique ethos tailored for hyper-local, on-demand couture in historic cultural epicenters like Nagpur (Sitabuldi, Gandhibagh, Dharampeth, Sadar). The aesthetic pivots from crowded discount marketplaces to an intentional, serene, and tactile atelier experience. 

The target audience consists of discerning fashion patrons seeking immediate bespoke garments, heritage handlooms, and designer couture delivered within hours. The visual style merges **Minimalism** and subtle **Glassmorphism** against deep, luminous warm alabaster foundations. Garment imagery acts as the primary centerpiece, framed by translucent warm porcelain panes, razor-thin graphite lines, deep obsidian typographic contrast, and restrained touches of antique gold and vivid crimson.

## Colors

The palette establishes an airy, museum-grade ivory environment ("Ivory Studio") accented with high-fashion jewel tones.

- **Ground & Structural Foundations**: `ground-base` (`#FAF9F5`) and `ground-subtle` (`#F4F3EE`) form the warm, tactile backdrop that prevents the stark, clinical chill of pure digital whites.
- **Surfaces & Glass**: Elevated cards use Pure Porcelain (`#FFFFFF`) or frosted glass (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)`) bounded by gossamer hairlines (`#E5E3DC` or `rgba(18, 18, 20, 0.08)`).
- **Typography Tiers**: `text-obsidian` (`#121215`) commands highest contrast; `text-slate` (`#4A4950`) carries narrative descriptions; `text-ash` (`#7E7C85`) handles strikethrough prices, auxiliary timestamps, and captioning.
- **Accents & Semantics**: 
  - `accent-crimson` (`#C4243A`) serves as the solitary primary interaction trigger (Bag CTA, checkout, real-time urgency).
  - `accent-crimson-deep` (`#8E1B29`) handles destructive flows and secondary pressed states.
  - `accent-gold` (`#B38A2B`) functions as a rare editorial signal, marking provenance, artisan origins, and hyper-local geographical proximities (e.g., Dharampeth • 1.8 km).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the literary grace of **EB Garamond** for editorial display headlines with the clean, contemporary clarity of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for body copy and metadata.

- **Editorial Serifs**: Display and Title styles set in EB Garamond capture classical Indian luxury craftsmanship and high-fashion editorial sensitivity.
- **Eyebrows & Provenance**: Category tags, artisan badges, and locality markers are locked to `eyebrow` styling: strict uppercase, semi-bold, tracked wide at `+0.25em`, and rendered in antique gold.
- **Tabular Figures**: All monetary amounts (`₹ INR`), delivery timing estimates (`mins away`), and geographical proximities use fixed-width numerical figure settings (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums;`) to maintain clean baseline and column alignment across high-density commerce grids.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a fluid layout model anchored to portrait mobile-first ergonomics, scaling gracefully into wider, curated viewports.

- **Base Rhythm**: A foundational 4px/8px incremental scale governed by named layout units: `xs` (6px), `sm` (12px), `md` (20px), `lg` (32px), and `xl` (48px).
- **Mobile Grid**: A single fluid vertical container with fixed `20px` (`gutter-md`) lateral margins. Full-bleed hero visuals extend to 62% of viewport height on product views before intersecting with floating cards.
- **Adaptive Reflow**: 
  - *Mobile (<768px)*: Single-column storefront rails, edge-to-edge photography with docked translucent action bars.
  - *Tablet & Desktop (≥768px)*: Max container width constrained to `1080px` centered with `48px` margins, converting garment rails into a balanced 3-column asymmetric editorial gallery.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects harsh, opaque drop shadows in favor of ambient multi-layer diffusion and frosted glass layering:

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas)**: Flat Warm Alabaster (`#FAF9F5`), devoid of elevation.
- **Level 1 (Recessed Inputs)**: Ground Subtle (`#F4F3EE`) with a hairline border (`rgba(18, 18, 20, 0.08)`) and an inset contact shadow (`inset 0 1px 2px rgba(18, 18, 20, 0.04)`).
- **Level 2 (Editorial Cards & Floating Panes)**: Pure Porcelain (`#FFFFFF`) or 75% translucent white frosted glass backed by a 20px Gaussian blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`). Framed with a uniform 1px hairline border (`#E5E3DC`) and an ambient, warm-tinted shadow: `0 12px 32px rgba(18, 18, 20, 0.05)`.
- **Level 3 (Docked Chrome & Action Bars)**: Translucent glass floating sheets pinned to screen perimeters, utilizing a higher diffusion drop shadow: `0 20px 48px rgba(18, 18, 20, 0.09)`.

## Shapes

The shape language reflects architectural tailoring: soft, restrained, and purposeful.

- **Corners**: The system sits at soft roundedness (`roundedness: 1`), utilizing `4px` on micro elements, `8px` on input fields, selection chips, and status tags, scaling up to `12px` on structural product cards and bottom sheets.
- **Pill Exceptions**: Pure circular capsules (`border-radius: 9999px`) are strictly reserved for functional touchpoints: floating utility icons (Back, Wishlist, Bag), cart badges, and the primary accent CTA.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Full capsule (`9999px`), solid Crimson Bright (`#C4243A`) background with white tabular typography. Elevates with a gentle scale dip to `0.97` upon active press.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Pure Porcelain or translucent glass with a 1px hairline graphite border (`rgba(18, 18, 20, 0.08)`), Obsidian text, and no drop shadow.
- **Icon Buttons**: 44×44px circular frosted glass surfaces with hairline outlines, housing minimalist thin-stroke iconography (1.5px stroke width).

### Chips & Selectors
- **Size & Filter Chips**: 8px rounded corners, Ground Subtle (`#F4F3EE`) background, 1px border. When selected, the background transitions to Pure Obsidian (`#121215`) with Crisp White typography, accompanied by a precise selection haptic.

### Cards
- **Product & Boutique Cards**: 12px corner radii, Pure Porcelain backing or frosted glass. Includes a 1:1.3 editorial portrait aspect ratio for garment imagery, an antique gold eyebrow badge for locality (e.g., "SADAR ATELIER"), an EB Garamond title, and tabular figures for price and proximity.

### Input Fields
- **Search & Concierge Inputs**: 8px corner radii, recessed Ground Subtle fill, 1px hairline graphite border. Placeholder text in Ash (`#7E7C85`), moving to active focus via an Obsidian hairline border without color glow.

### Badges & Status Indicators
- **Proximity & Provenance Pills**: Subtle ivory background with Antique Gold typography and a faint gold border (`rgba(179, 138, 43, 0.25)`).
- **Express Urgency Badges**: Micro-capsule pills containing a Crimson Bright core dot, signaling same-day Nagpur dispatch.
